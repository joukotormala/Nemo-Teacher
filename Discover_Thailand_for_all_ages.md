## 🧭 Educational Themes for Students

- **Geography & Ecosystems:** Coral reefs (Surin Islands), savanna ecosystems (Koh Phra Thong), limestone karst formations (Phang Nga), tropical rainforest springs (Surat Thani), and alpine pine highlands (Phu Soi Dao).
- **Culture & History:** Chao Phraya river communities, Yaowarat Chinatown history, Moken indigenous sea nomads, and Wat Pha Sorn Kaew temple architecture.
- **Wildlife & Conservation:** Dugong preservation in Trang, sea turtle habitats in the Andaman Sea, and tropical marine biodiversity.
